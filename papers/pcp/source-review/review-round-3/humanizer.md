## Paragraph decisions

- Long opening: put the full endpoint first, then the assembled verifier. Remove the second full theorem statement that relocation would duplicate. Keep the proof-before-randomness and language-before-input quantifiers.
- Long imports: identify the callback, compilation, reduction and converse results by what they supply. The table limits the claimed explanatory coverage without turning the opening into a validation report.
- Long testing proofs: retain the formal left inverse, nearest-line tie argument, four mixed-plane errors, five-error surface estimate and fresh-mask coefficient. These repeated distinctions carry mathematical conditions.
- Specialist opening: state the theorem and the two encoding operations directly. Replace the claim that constant-bit correction recovers a field value by the binary observation it actually returns.
- Specialist Section 2: split the cubic equivalence from padding and interpolation; introduce the eight received tables and their types before using them. The compact table supports an actual comparison.
- Specialist Section 4: separate the point-line contract from its historical attribution and proof destinations. Keep the complete fixed-target correction argument in Section 5. State the conditional row error before averaging it over the table.
- Exposition Sections 4–8: reuse the same one-edge colouring, explain each new address domain, and carry its vertex certificate through the weighted reads. Remove the separate F5 certificate calculation; retain the F5 digit example because it demonstrates a different operation without a finite-field degree collapse.
- Exposition degree explanation: correct the reason for the Boolean quadratic bound. Squaring is binary-linear in every characteristic-two finite field; the phenomenon is not specific to two binary coordinates. This mathematical change was made before the bounded language pass.
- Exposition ending: leave the reconstruction task and its answers in the main route. Move detailed parameter arithmetic, Boolean gadgets and historical test failure to appendices. Keep the brief polynomial-resource and reduction explanations in the argument.
- Preserve standard terms such as homogeneous label, formal polynomial and rejection probability. Do not rotate synonyms to satisfy a stylistic metric. Preserve the fixed-proof reminder at repetition and the same-witness reminders at each consumer; these are useful local hypotheses.
