# Paragraph decisions and replacement prose

This is a consequential-paragraph review of the frozen long record, not a claim that every prose block needed alteration. The ledger covers every section and more than forty distinct prose paragraphs. Exact short quotations locate the paragraph; source line ranges are edition-bound. A retained paragraph is an affirmative decision. P1 means repair before reuse; P2 means a useful local explanatory revision; P3 means polish or an explicitly optional addition. These are proposed edits, not incorporated changes.

The replacements preserve hypotheses, quantifiers, source credit and evidence boundaries. Formula notation is written in a readable mathematical form; the editor should preserve the manuscript's existing TeX macros and link syntax. Rebind affected reader selections and hashes after any actual edit.

## R1 — Formal left inverse before evaluation (priority 1)

**Source:** pcp-long-set-multilinear, lines 788–803. Move the existing definition at 797–801 before the inference at 795; replace the surrounding transition by the following passage. Keep the earlier Enc and Φ definitions and the later decoded-degree bound.

For any formal polynomial Q in the variables X_(i,k), define
\[
\operatorname{Dec}(Q)(Y)=Q\bigl((Y^{k b^i})_{i,k}\bigr).
\]
The digit-monomial calculation holds before evaluating Y: substituting Y^(k b^i) into the encoding of Y^j gives Y^(Σ_i j_i b^i)=Y^j. By linearity,
