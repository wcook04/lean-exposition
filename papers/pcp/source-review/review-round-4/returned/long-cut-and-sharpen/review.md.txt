# Third review, Lens A: cut and sharpen

## Result

This return supplies a complete proposed revision of the primary paper, with a disposition for every baseline block and paste-ready replacements. The revision has **21,671 words**, down from **23,276**, a net reduction of **1,605 words (6.90%)** under the packet's counting rule. There is no net-growth exception. The 498 kept blocks remain byte-identical; 144 blocks have proposed changes. All 173 display-math blocks, 55 equation labels and 146 passage markers survive unchanged and in order.

The main opportunity is to remove repeated explanation of the proof's organisation. The calculations and local hypotheses generally earn their space. The opening gives the route several times; later paragraphs often paraphrase a displayed estimate or defend a limitation that the preceding quantified statement already expresses. Cutting those sentences makes the proof easier to follow without turning the long record into another specialist summary.

The proposed text includes three mathematically sensitive wording or scope repairs, isolated in `math_flags.md`: the affine line-table address label, the repeated-row case in the cofactor explanation, and the generic degree parameter used in the assembly contracts. They require the packet's native semantic check before adoption. They do not change a displayed formula or numerical constant, and this review identifies no new theorem-level defect in the inspected formal statements.

## 4. Keep the calculations that carry the proof

The following material is deliberately protected:

- **One simultaneous decoding.** The same P, Q_*, M_A and M_B are retained through the difference and grid checks. Full-label and homogeneous-label bounds concern one decoded object. An arbitrary auxiliary table is never assumed honest or close in advance.
- **Both degree regimes.** Honest outer polynomials have degree strictly below the digit threshold. An arbitrary decoded inner polynomial may produce the larger outer degree bound. The derived constraints require the common 5D bound in the assembly.
- **The weighted test's complete hypotheses.** The full multiplicative generator, Lagrange weights, strict rejection threshold and field assumptions remain. The full statement near b0292 was required by an earlier review and is retained.
- **The clean-rectangle and cofactor derivation.** The goal before the kernel construction, sampling estimates, nonzero minor, degree control, exact agreement and boosting calculations remain. These are proof steps, not repeated motivation.
- **The ruled-surface argument.** Its independent samples, good/bad restrictions, fixed polynomial choices, dimension and degree boundary cases, and quantitative constants remain.
- **Correction at nonuniform targets.** Both the dependence on actual rejection and averaging over the requested homogeneous label remain. No fixed-label reliability claim is substituted for an averaged one.
- **The actual ZERO procedure.** It uses the same certificate tables, two correction calls sharing the outer point and label, and independent remaining seeds. The fresh-mask loss retains the multiplier 1+2C(c).
- **The machine bridge.** The bounded callback semantics, all-answer-history correctness, explicit program and cost data, polynomial graph construction, resource transport and concrete/reference NP equivalence remain.
- **Exact resources and small cases.** The least-parameter argument, eight-table address count, seven seed exponents, four whole-field draws, fixed repetition, strict inequalities and zero-degree cases remain.
- **The unbounded-field counterexample.** The old scalar test's failure is established for arbitrarily large fields. Its scope remains the old scalar characterization and soundness lemma; it is not presented as a cheating proof against all seven checks.

The query cap at c=4 remains **at most 187 physical reads per unamplified run**. The query list length for the inner Hadamard test remains exactly 2r+4, with repeated addresses distinguished from distinct locations. These are different assertions and the revision keeps the distinction.

## 5. Give each companion a purpose without deleting necessary setup

The long account owns complete derivations and the machine-model composition. The specialist paper owns the compressed path through the construction. The exposition owns worked calculations and pedagogical examples. Each needs its theorem, the relevant table inventory and enough definitions to be read independently.

Consequently, the repeated field setup, certificate identities, query procedure and same-witness requirement are usually justified across papers. Deleting them from the long argument would make the proof depend on its companions. The redundancy map identifies these as deliberate keeps and gives their exact locations. Near-verbatim setup is not automatically unnecessary when it is the point at which each independent paper defines its objects.

The long manuscript alone is changed in this return. No new production system, external review gate or live-site change is proposed.

## 6. Apply the bounded humanizer to mathematical prose

The principal patterns found are announcements, repeated scope notes, empty transitions, defensive contrasts and source narration. Examples include “Here is the precise version”, “We next relate”, “The remaining task”, and caveats repeated immediately after a quantified statement. Replacements begin with the definition, condition or inference that the reader needs.

The pass does not replace technical vocabulary by less exact synonyms, add promotional adjectives, impose a voice on a theorem statement, or remove difficult reasoning to make the prose seem conversational. Bold emphasis on “same” and on the final rejection conclusion is unnecessary when the quantifiers and conclusion are already explicit. An internal node-condition name is replaced by the mathematical condition already defined. Visible revision hashes are removed from bibliography link labels while immutable source URLs remain intact.

`humanizer.md` records the actual before/after passages. There is no detector score and no fabricated finding for a pattern absent from the text.
