## MF1 — Coefficient count (b0012)

**Before:** “a line polynomial's coefficient vector takes its degree times as many.”

**Proposed sentence:** “In a field of size $2^t$, a field value takes $t$ bits, and the coefficient vector of a line polynomial of degree at most $d$ takes $(d+1)t$ bits.”

**Content affected:** the exact number of coefficients, d+1 rather than d. The asymptotic bit-cost obstruction is unchanged.

**Evidence:** v2 intro.tex:27 gives the coefficient alphabet $F_q^{d+1}$; preliminaries.tex:225,233 identifies this representation of a degree-at-most-d univariate polynomial. The exact introductory sentence cannot use an asymptotic omission of the additive one as a literal count.

**History:** LIT-02's retained distinction between bit cost and proof length stays in place. This corrects current wording without reopening that allocation.

## MF2 — Upper bounds rather than exact degree (b0014, b0062, b0093)

**Before:** “a degree-$c$ polynomial”; “The inner polynomial has constant degree $c$”; “a degree-$D$ polynomial $P$.”

**After:** respectively “a polynomial of total degree at most $c$”; “The inner degree bound is the constant $c$”; and “Let $P$ have total degree at most $D\ge cb^c$.”

**Content affected:** literal degree equality is removed. Mathematical prose sometimes uses “degree-d” to mean degree at most d; this is conservatively flagged so that convention is not doing hidden work.

**Evidence:** SetMultilinear.lean:149–151, totalDegree_encode_le, is unconditional. Lines 153–155, totalDegree_encode, require P≠0 for equality. GeneratorLowDegreeTest.lean:274–292 concludes totalDegree≤c. BinaryLocalCorrectionSoundness.lean:222–236 has both hP:P.totalDegree≤D and hdecode:c*m^c≤D.

**Native check:** confirm the same upper bounds and the strict honest bound below b^c; no bound is to be replaced by the other. The source's m in the inner encoding is the specialist paper's digit base b.

**History:** the honest/decoded distinction retained under ALG-11 is preserved. The current b0042 already says “at most c”; the change makes the introductory shorthand agree with it.

## MF3 — The missing restriction guarantee is uniform in t (b0060)

**Before:** “Full-label proximity does not by itself control the homogeneous restriction.”

**After:** “For $r>1$, full-label proximity alone gives no bound on homogeneous error by a constant multiple independent of $t$.”

**Content affected:** the old absolute statement is narrowed. When r=1, homogeneous error is at most twice affine/full error. For any fixed finite domain, a restriction bound exists with the reciprocal of the subdomain's relative size.

**Evidence:** HadamardAffineQueries.lean:60–79, homogeneousDistance_le_twice_affine. HadamardRowDecoding.lean:34–54 uses it for the same decoded value. The old paper's own b0070 already uses the factor-two deduction.

**Ordinary diagnostic calculation:** let $d_r(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(r,t)}\binom tj$. The full label space has $2^{d_r(t)}$ labels and its homogeneous subspace has $2^t$. Flip an evaluation word exactly on the homogeneous subspace. Its full error is $2^{t-d_r(t)}$ and its homogeneous error is one. For fixed r>1, the first tends to zero as t grows. This calculation only explains the qualified wording; it is not added to the manuscript as a new result.

**History:** ALG-02's repaired domain distinction remains. The new evidence is the conflict between the current absolute sentence and the retained degree-one inequality; the earlier decoding result is not challenged.
