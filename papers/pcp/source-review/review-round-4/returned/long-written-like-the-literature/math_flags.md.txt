## M1. Linear coordinates and the additive label class

**Blocks:** b0051; later correct definitions at b0430–b0431 and b0456–b0457.  
**Class:** local missing hypothesis and ambiguous domain.  
**Confidence:** high; direct source and formal declarations, with an elementary counterexample to the overbroad reading.

The original first-use paragraph specifies only a coordinate map and describes homogeneous labels through their zero constant term. Sum preservation needs an $\mathbb F_2$-linear coordinate map and homogeneous **linear** labels. For example, $R(z_1,z_2)=z_1z_2$ has zero constant term and is homogeneous of degree two, but

$$
R((1,0)+(0,1))=1\ne0=R(1,0)+R(0,1).
$$

The intended degree-one subfamily is defined correctly later. The replacement makes that intended restriction explicit at first use and calls $\rho$ an $\mathbb F_2$-linear isomorphism.

**Evidence:** ABSSW v2, `technical_tools.tex` 119–143, §3.3, before Definition 3.5; `preliminaries.tex` 68–70; `formal/lean/PCP/Algebraic/Hadamard.lean` 137–188, `linearEncode`, `homogeneousIndex`, `polynomial_homogeneousIndex`, `relativeDistance_linearEncode`; `BinaryDifferenceConsistency.check_encode`.

**Adoption check:** the chosen binary coordinates must be the same linear isomorphism later used by the weighted relabelling and difference tests. Every sum-preserving observation must be in the homogeneous degree-one subfamily. No encoding, distance constant or query count changes.

## M5. Compare distances on their actual domains

**Block:** b0555.  
**Class:** precision correction to a source-strength comparison; neither theorem is disproved.  
**Confidence:** high; direct probability identity and source statements.

The original calls the source's affine-label conclusion “stronger” without qualifying constants or testers. Let $e_0$ and $e_1$ be error rates on homogeneous labels and their constant-one translates. Then

$$
e_{\rm aff}=(e_0+e_1)/2,\qquad e_0\le2e_{\rm aff}.
$$

The same numerical bound is not ordered by domain inclusion. For $t=r=3$, there are 256 full labels, 16 affine labels and eight homogeneous labels. Flipping precisely the eight constant-one affine labels of an exact codeword gives full/affine/homogeneous errors $(1/32,1/2,0)$. Flipping one homogeneous label gives $(1/256,1/16,1/8)$. These examples concern distances only; they do not assign a rejection rate or contradict a tester theorem.

ABSSW uses $O_c(\delta)$, which absorbs the factor two. Its affine conclusion does asymptotically control the homogeneous restriction and additional labels. The replacement names the two domains and the valid factor-two relation rather than making an unqualified numerical comparison.

**Evidence:** ABSSW v2 Theorem 4.1, `low_degree_test.tex` 219–247; `EncodedLineLowDegreeSoundness.lean` 325–339, `EncodedLineLowDegreeTest.exists_close`; `HadamardAffineQueries.lean` and the homogeneous restriction definitions.

**Adoption check:** preserve both simultaneous conclusions for the same decoded polynomial, the exact $S(c)$ bound in (B15), and the certificate's full affine-domain guarantee. No numerical constant in the verifier changes.

## M6. Expose averaging already required by the correct statements

**Blocks:** b0592, b0607–b0609.  
**Class:** probability-scope clarification, not a changed inequality.  
**Confidence:** high; fixed-target and joint-distribution declarations.

At b0592, apply (B16) at each requested target using its own rejection probability $R_a$, then average over the sampled $x$. A correction-call rejection implies ZERO rejection in that joint experiment, so the average is at most $\kappa$. There is no assertion that $R_a\le\kappa$ separately at every target.

At b0607, the two downstream tests share the same uniform point, requested label and other randomness. Their decisions differ only when the masked derived bit differs from its ideal value. Conditional on the point and label, the error is bounded by twice that row's error; averaging gives $2\epsilon$. The fresh mask stays in the joint random seed throughout.

**Evidence:** `BinaryLocalCorrectionSoundness.lean` 222–236; `BinaryZeroOnGridSoundness.lean` 210–267; `HadamardDerivedOracle.lean` 68–118 and 149–178; `DerivedZeroOnGridSoundness.lean` 187–194.

**Adoption check:** retain uniformity of the outer point and freshness of the mask, all dependencies of requested labels, (B24), and the factor $1+2C(c)$ multiplying the replacement loss in (B25). The source text already has the correct displayed bounds; the replacement makes the averaging order harder to misread.
