## M01 — make the ordinary certificate condition explicit

**Block:** b0006.

**Current scope:** “every problem whose proposed solutions can be checked in polynomial time.” This leaves the ordinary witness-length bound and the equivalence between acceptance and membership implicit. The next sentence bounds the encoded PCP certificate, which is a different object.

**Proposed scope:** every decision problem whose yes-instances are exactly those having a polynomial-length certificate accepted by a deterministic polynomial-time checker. The complete replacement is in `replacements.md`.

**Evidence:**

- `formal/lean/PCP/Complexity/NP.lean:31–43`, `NPCertificate`: `witnessBound` is a polynomial; `check_polyTime` supplies the algorithm condition; `spec` is the biconditional between membership and an accepted witness whose length is bounded by `witnessBound.eval x.length`.
- `formal/lean/PCP/Theorem.lean:25–33`, `np_subset_pcp`, instantiates the concrete polynomial-time relation.
- Arora–Barak, January 2007 draft, Definition 2.1, PDF page 56 / printed p.2.2 (40), gives the corresponding certificate formulation.
- Current b0019 already states the intended NP condition. The precise theorem at b0021–b0022 is unaffected.

**Effect:** explicitly supplies a mathematical hypothesis and characterisation already required by the cited native definition. It does not add a theorem or change the proved scope.

**Native-check status:** the actual definition and theorem interface were opened and compared. No Lean source was modified or compiled. The native adoption check requested by the packet should confirm this wording against the existing definition; no proof amendment is proposed.

## Settled mathematics retained

The cubic label space is retained even though some characteristic-two derived observations have degree at most two. The sharp BCHKS rejection-versus-distance inequality is retained. The fixed-anchor retest, rejection-inclusive corrector bound, fresh masks, independent remaining ZERO seeds conditional on the shared point and label, and all same-polynomial requirements remain. No earlier decision is reopened as a new proof failure.

The old-test counterexample remains a finite illustration plus an ordinary root-bound argument over an unbounded family of fields. It is not relabelled as a theorem of the Lean development. The finite Python checks and exact rational arithmetic do not certify the general PCP theorem.
