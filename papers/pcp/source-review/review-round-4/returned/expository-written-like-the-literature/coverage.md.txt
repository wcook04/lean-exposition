## What this review does not establish

- No Lean build, Comparator run, axiom audit or independent replay of the proof dependency graph was performed. Earlier supplied build/comparator receipts were not represented as fresh executions.
- The review compared source statements, definitions, algorithms and relevant proof explanations. It did not independently reconstruct the entire PCP proof or read all pages of all 23 literature PDFs.
- The original texts of the unacquired DeMillo–Lipton, Ore and Schwartz bibliography items were not read. Their names were not promoted into checked citations. The available ABSSW polynomial-distance theorem supplies the local reference.
- The small finite examples do not satisfy every asymptotic production parameter and are not general soundness proofs. The counterexample family in Appendix C is an ordinary mathematical deduction, outside the formalisation.
- There was no independent human reader study, human mathematical endorsement or measured learning outcome. This is a model review with separately delegated passes.
- No original manuscript, Lean source or live site was changed; nobody was contacted. No credentials, mailbox or private research service were accessed. The response is a review package with a candidate source copy.
- No revised PDF was typeset, so new line breaks, figure placement and pagination were not assessed. The requested review files and paste-ready source replacements are complete.
