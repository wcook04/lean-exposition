# Review one explanation

Choose one reader, one exact edition and one question. The aim of a first session
is to find where an explanation helps or fails, then make a source-faithful revision.
A favourable impression, a proof check and demonstrated understanding are different
observations. None substitutes for the others.

## Judge the whole paper against its purpose

Use the [three paper purposes](authoring.md#what-makes-each-kind-of-paper-good)
before assigning local edits. Read the current paper in sequence at its declared
prerequisite level; record prior exposure to the argument and reviews. An author
or agent can assess a route but cannot report an unassisted human reading or a
learning gain that did not occur.

For the specialist paper, recover the result, contribution and decisive proof
without asking the long record to supply its central idea. For the exposition,
trace how examples and explanations equip the reader for the promised operation
and a changed case. For the long record, trace both the continuous argument and
entry at a substantial proof, including its premises and return to the result
it supports. Retain actual passages or responses as evidence.

Assess hierarchy as well as local clarity. Identify the passages carrying the
main insight and the space devoted to their suppliers. Check whether each
difficult device arrives after its need becomes intelligible, whether the ending
pays off the opening question, and whether the account's claimed independence
matches its dependencies. Uniform detail can conceal the hard step; repeated
previews can conceal an absent explanation. More signposting alone repairs neither.

Apply the [literature and language review](authoring.md#mathematical-register-and-literature-grounded-prose)
to each paper. Compare passages doing the same explanatory job in the actual
literature. At the predicted hard step, say what this reader knows on arrival
and try the inference using only that knowledge and the passage. At a passage
devoted to familiar material, try removing it or giving its exact result and
citation. What could the reader no longer understand, apply or reconstruct?
Keep the explanation if the answer identifies a necessary condition, a delicate
application or the very operation this paper promises to teach. Otherwise
release the space to the harder step. Check that the field's terms remain stable
and that fluency does not hide a missing inference. Read the full revised text
sequentially after a humanizer pass;
a phrase scan cannot establish natural mathematical prose. Check mathematical
meaning again where wording changed. Keep this evidence outside the paper.

When several reviews describe the same difficulty, synthesize their diagnosis
before accepting their proposed additions. Test the revised opening, argument
and ending against the [per-type deliverables](authoring.md#shared-structure-different-allocation).
Record which earlier criticism no longer applies and why; do not merge
incompatible recommendations or turn the manuscript into a response to reviewers.

Test composition separately from the presence of explanations. At a difficult
construction, can the reader identify the question it answers from the preceding
text? After a central proof, can they state what has been established and use
it in the next argument? Check whether a supporting derivation obscures that
transition, or whether moving it would conceal the very mechanism this paper
promises to explain. Read the revised transition in the delivered PDF, including
its page break and any intervening figure. A source-level move alone does not
establish a better reading sequence.

When the proof turns on a choice, test what the preceding passage makes
possible. Give the obstacle, available information and constraints. Withhold
the proposed object, method name and formula. Ask what the reader would try
and what it must preserve. Accept another choice if its justification meets
the same requirements. A calculation done after revealing the choice supports
a narrower judgment. If the reader has already seen it, use a changed problem
that calls for a fresh choice, or record independent selection as unassessed.
Then follow the paper's actual choice through its first use and any later step
that relies on the same property. Check that an optional refinement does not
interrupt that connection.
Check that the main worked example actually exhibits the operation or improvement
the text promises, and distinguish this job from illustrating a failure case.

Choose the most consequential remaining obstacle and a concrete revision that
would remove it. State what the reader should then be able to recover, and what
must survive the edit. Reorder, replace or relocate before accumulating another
layer of explanation. Re-read the complete affected route after editing, rather
than inspecting only new sentences. Missing mathematics returns to research;
clarity cannot certify an unsupported implication.

Close with a bounded judgment of the delivered work: which reader tasks are now
supported, what evidence was inspected, and which consequential difficulty
remains or was not assessed. Keep mathematical validity, explanatory coherence,
navigation and observed reader performance distinct. Scores may summarize an
opinion, but repeated scores do not diagnose a cause and a self-assigned perfect
score does not establish excellence. Local issue counts cannot close this review.

## Freeze what the reader will see

Use the [bundle identity convention](bundle-consumers.md#identify-a-revision-before-requesting-review).
Retain the bundle, the actual PDF or other rendition, and a short request recording:

- the edition, paper and passages to read;
- the intended reader's stated background and any prerequisites supplied;
- the question and approximate time available;
- what is outside this review, such as a complete proof audit or formal replay;
- any source or receipt the reader cannot access.

Keep the request, original response and subsequent author decisions outside the
eight-file bundle. A request is a plan. Until a response exists, record the reading
as unperformed; silence does not report either understanding or rejection.
Identify an author check, model or agent analysis, and an independent person's
attempt separately. A model's worked answers and simulated reading difficulties
can inform revision; they do not record human understanding or learning gains.

Keep the first-account prompt, later diagnostic questions and assessor answer key
in separate files or separately released pages. Prepare a blank response sheet and
an assistance log before use. Record the actual last passage read and any worked
answers already seen; a planned reading range does not establish exposure. A new
numerical instance may test use of a taught mechanism, but changing numbers does
not make a previously exposed solution fresh.

## Pick a route

For a specialist, a useful bounded question is whether the result, hard step,
assumptions and literature credit are recoverable from the short paper. Ask for
the first unsupported transition and its source destination. Reading the short
paper is not an audit of every source linked from it.

For someone learning the subject, choose one section of the exposition. After
reading, ask them to explain its result and argument in their own words with the
text closed. Save that first account before giving hints or a checklist. Then
offer one fresh example or changed hypothesis appropriate to what they read.
Finally reopen the text and ask which passage resolves, or fails to resolve,
their uncertainty. Record skipped sections as unassessed.

During a chronological reading, record the first point where the reader needs
a missing definition, purpose or intermediate inference. Let them interrupt at
that point before supplying context from a later section. Keep that observation
separate from a later reconstruction after help; it identifies a sequencing
problem that a fluent whole-paper summary can conceal.

Freeze the first-pass file before source checks or later annotation; record its
digest and the materials already seen, including inherited critique and answers.
Append resolutions with source locators instead of rewriting the encounter.
Label a retrospectively expanded sentence index as such. Inspect its boundaries:
formulas, captions and nonrendered source markers are different coverage units.
An exhaustive index does not establish contemporaneous observations, independent
readers or learning gains. Record the actual order and assistance rather than
silently replacing an unperformed protocol with a similar one.

For a strict prospective pass, reveal one semantic unit, save its encounter
record, and only then reveal the next. Include captions and generated references
at their positions in the delivered edition. If the interface reveals a whole
paragraph or page, record that exposure; ordered annotations within that block
do not establish isolated sentence encounters. This stricter procedure is useful
when the question concerns first-use dependencies, not a requirement for every
bounded proof review.

The QAOA/SK example has several separable tasks: understanding support and an
atom; reconstructing the endpoint-and-convexity contradiction; distinguishing
first and second moments; and explaining the ordered limits of the QAOA claim.
Only ask a task after the corresponding material has been read. An answer about
the no-gap argument cannot establish comprehension of the circuit construction.

Match each promised outcome to a task that would reveal it: recognition,
following a taught procedure and reconstructing a proof are different outcomes.
Replace redundant tasks before adding more. In a worked answer, show the short
calculation that distinguishes the plausible mistake, including whether a
conclusion is false or merely unsupported by the proposed bound. Simple recall
questions need no extended diagnosis.

Match each task to the inference in the edition being read. After a proof-route
revision, keep useful tasks for unchanged steps and replace or supplement those
that miss the new step. If an easy example makes distinct quantities equal,
use a nearby case that separates them. Check the other hypotheses and the worked
answer first; an arbitrary broken example may test a different failure.
Use the [whole-paper review](#judge-the-whole-paper-against-its-purpose) to
test selection when the passage promises it. Keep a changed case that separates
a false conclusion, insufficient information and failure of the proposed route.

## Preserve the attempt, including assistance

Record the first response before any correction. Keep later hints, answer exposure
and revised responses in order. Assess the result, conditions, proof mechanism,
changed case and scope separately. Do not average a missing essential assumption
away with correct arithmetic. Preserve unexpected valid reasoning; if it contradicts
the proposed answer key, inspect the source before marking the reader wrong.

Use separate facet observations with supporting response excerpts, not an aggregate
pass score. Preserve an unassessed state for unread or interrupted material. Review
each key against the current edition: a later theorem may strengthen a conclusion
that an earlier task deliberately did not assume.

Ask about difficulty and preferences separately from reconstruction. A repeated
attempt after explanation measures something different from a first reading.
Better performance by the same reader after a revision cannot by itself identify
the revision as its cause.

This is an author-designed formative review, not a validated test or a claim of
learning effectiveness. Davies, Alcock and Jones studied expert judgments of
undergraduate summaries of an uncountability proof; their discussion distinguishes
summary quality from other assessments and their analysis attends to the objects
and contradiction mechanism. That is a useful comparison for our questions, not
validation of an SK instrument. See their [2020 paper, §§3.1,4.3,5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10649-020-09984-x).

## Revise the source and keep the original review

For each actionable observation, retain the disputed passage, the relevant source,
the author's decision and its reason. A source error can be corrected directly;
a preference may call for a different route or no change. Do not decide mathematical
truth by counting agreeing reports.

Before diagnosing a mathematical error, state the exact required contract and
check the current operation's cases, including tie rules and early termination.
A familiar implementation may impose stronger requirements than this consumer
needs. A finite calculation may refute that criticism without proving the
general theorem. Preserve the initial uncertainty and explicitly withdraw it
when resolved; do not convert it silently into a different complaint.

Follow the [authoring contract](authoring.md#track-assumptions-and-citations-through-a-rewrite)
through the long record, short paper, exposition and their mappings. Rebuild the
bundle and inspect the delivered rendition. Record the successor's digest and
actual checks. An author reporting a fix is distinct from a reader confirming it.
If an earlier acceptance no longer describes the changed content, reopen that
review. Preserve its original edition, question and actual coverage.

A reviewer may ask for more traceability while the reader needs less visible
administration. Resolve the underlying need rather than copying the proposed
presentation. Recheck the actual PDF after the move: can the reader state the
mathematical scope without interpreting internal names, and can they follow a
natural-language link to both the formal statement and its substantive proof?
An unchanged rule in an authoring guide is not evidence that this release
applied it. Keep this acceptance observation with the exact revised rendition.

Close the authoring pass with the
[review-and-refinement loop](authoring.md#use-and-improve-the-transition-skills).
Locate the decision that caused the difficulty, then distinguish an existing
rule that was missed, a missing reusable instruction and a choice specific to
this paper. A new instruction needs transfer cases and an exception before it
enters the responsible transition skill. Keep the original reader observation
separate from this author's diagnosis and from a later confirmation of the fix.

No person has completed a session merely because these instructions or an answer
key exist. Report an actual attempt only from the retained response and assistance
record. Preparing the materials does not deliver a review request.
